healthy public housing

illustration
editorial design

For this project, I worked closely with the team at Got Green to translate the lived experience of hundreds of public housing tenants into data and illustrations that capture not only the current reality of public housing in Seattle but also the potential of a healthier future and what it would take to achieve this. The end result was a 48-page report with 18 custom illustrations, plus infographics, a campaign logo, and summarized graphic booklets translated into the most common languages spoken in South Seattle.

View the full report here: gotgreenseattle.org/publications
