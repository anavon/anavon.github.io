poverty action

editorial design

Poverty Action is an organization that advocates for low-income people in Washington state, listening to community needs and bringing these concerns to legislature, with the goal of advancing policies that eliminate root causes of poverty and expand financial safety nets.

In both 2020 and 2022, I worked with Poverty Action to design and illustrate printed Policy Agendas outlining ongoing projects and goals of the organization, as well as producing additional illustrations for the Poverty Action website.
