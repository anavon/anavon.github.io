community stewardship of land

illustration

In 2021, I was commissioned by a coalition of local organizations to produce illustrations for a new website dedicated to their shared goal of community land stewardship. These illustrations depict possibilities available through collective land ownership (vs. for-profit corporations or individual landlords), as well as steps needed to acquire and preserve this land within a community or neighborhood, especially in the face of threats like gentrification.
