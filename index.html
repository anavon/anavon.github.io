about

I've been working in the design field for over a decade, but I still love learning and creatively collaborating in new ways. I currently work as a Senior Graphic Designer for Planned Parenthood Great Northwest, Hawai‘i, Alaska, Indiana, Kentucky, in addition to doing freelance design and illustration work for other nonprofits, businesses, publishing houses, and local bands. In my free time, I play bass in a couple of these bands, and enjoy camping and hiking in the beautiful northwest. My professional experience includes brand identity, illustration, web design, packaging, and both print + digital promotion.
